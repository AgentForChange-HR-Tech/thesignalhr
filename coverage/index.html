Coverage

Ten domains. One lens.

Everything that decides HR's next decade, tracked continuously and filtered through one practitioner's judgment. Each domain below carries its current signal — updated as the landscape moves.

Updated July 23, 2026
HR Tech & Agentic AI

The platform landscape, the agent layer arriving on top of it, and what 'AI-first HCM' actually means operationally. We track vendor claims against deployed reality — which agents complete work, which are chatbots in a trench coat.

Current signal · Agentic AI is becoming a core HCM capability across the major suites; the differentiator is moving from features to governance — audit trails, override rights, and human oversight.
Talent Acquisition

Recruiting technology and the new hiring funnel: AI screening, conversational scheduling, candidate-side AI, and what structured hiring looks like when both sides use machines.

Current signal · With AI writing candidates' applications and screening them too, signal quality is the new battleground — structured interviews and work samples are back in force.
People Analytics

Workforce intelligence, skills data, and evidence-based HR decisions — plus the honest limits of prediction when the underlying work is being redesigned.

Current signal · The frontier is decision support for the agentic transition: which roles change first, where judgment concentrates, what to measure when output is machine-assisted.
Risk & Compliance

AI hiring regulation from the EU AI Act to US state law, plus algorithmic accountability, monitoring rules, and the governance HR now owns.

Current signal · The EU AI Act's high-risk HR obligations apply from August 2, 2026 — worker notice, human oversight, logging — with extraterritorial reach into US-run tools used on EU workers.
HR Transformation

Operating-model redesign for the agentic era: what HR keeps, what agents take, and how the function reorganizes around judgment rather than transaction processing.

Current signal · The rehiring boomerang — a third of AI-cut roles refilled — is the clearest evidence yet that workflow redesign must precede headcount decisions.
Future of Work

Scenarios and signals for the workforce of 2030: hybrid human–agent teams, the changing shape of careers, and what disappearing entry-level work means for talent pipelines.

Current signal · Entry-level employment in AI-exposed occupations is down ~13% since late 2022 — the on-ramp problem is becoming the decade's defining talent question.
L&D & Skills

Skills intelligence, learning platforms, and capability-building when the half-life of tool skills keeps shrinking.

Current signal · Skills-data consolidation (Lightcast's acquisition run; Docebo–365Talents) is centralizing the market's labor-market intelligence layer — watch pricing and lock-in.
Career Pathways

How HR careers themselves are being redrawn: emerging roles, the skills that compound, and honest maps for practitioners navigating the shift.

Current signal · Judgment, not tool fluency, is the compounding skill — 73% of talent leaders now rank critical thinking first; AI skills came fifth.
Mobility & Immigration

Global workforce movement for HR: policy shifts, compliance load, and the systems that make (or break) cross-border employment.

Current signal · EOR consolidation (Payoneer–Boundless, Remote–Atlas) is reshaping the global-employment stack — fewer vendors, deeper stakes per contract.
Vendor Comparisons

The independent buyer's lens on HR technology. Side-by-side matrices, best-fit guidance, and RFP questions — no vendor pays to appear, rank, or be described favorably.
