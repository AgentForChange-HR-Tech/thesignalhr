# Source registry

Where the scout and writer look first, by tier (see `_tools/PUBLISHING.md`, Gate 1). Open the page itself; a search snippet is never a source. Confirm a link still resolves before relying on it, and add better primary sources as you find them.

## Tier A: primary sources, used across domains

| Area | Sources |
| --- | --- |
| EU law | EUR-Lex (eur-lex.europa.eu); European Commission digital strategy and AI Office pages (digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu) |
| US federal | Federal Register (federalregister.gov); eCFR (ecfr.gov); EEOC (eeoc.gov); Department of Labor (dol.gov); court dockets via CourtListener (courtlistener.com) |
| US states | Each state legislature's own bill pages; state attorney general and civil-rights agency sites. LegiScan (legiscan.com) to track bills, then confirm on the legislature's page |
| UK | legislation.gov.uk; ICO (ico.org.uk); GOV.UK department pages |
| Canada | Justice Laws (laws-lois.justice.gc.ca); LEGISinfo (parl.ca/legisinfo); provincial legislatures and privacy commissioners |
| Asia-Pacific | OAIC (oaic.gov.au) and legislation.gov.au; MeitY (meity.gov.in); Singapore MOM (mom.gov.sg) and TAFEP (tal.sg/tafep); national data-protection authorities |
| Companies | The company's own newsroom, release notes, help center and product documentation; SEC EDGAR filings (sec.gov) for deals and financials |
| Labor data | BLS (bls.gov) including the Occupational Outlook Handbook; OECD (oecd.org); ILO (ilo.org); Eurostat (ec.europa.eu/eurostat) |

## Tier B: independent secondary sources

- Law firms' client alerts, where the firm and date are named (for example Ogletree Deakins, Littler, Fisher Phillips, Jackson Lewis, DLA Piper, Hunton, Morgan Lewis, Epstein Becker Green, Ius Laboris members). Prefer two that agree for any legal claim without a Tier A source.
- Established news and trade press with named reporters (for example Reuters, Bloomberg, HR Dive). Use the original study they report on when it's available.

## Tier C: context only, never the only support

Vendor marketing pages, analyst summaries and press-release recaps, blogs, social posts, newsletters and AI-generated summaries.

## By domain: what to scan each week

| Domain | Scan first | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HR Tech & Agentic AI | Vendors' newsrooms and release notes for every platform in the comparisons | Announced vs available; beta labels; pricing changes |
| Talent Acquisition | Vendor release notes; EEOC and state agencies; hiring-AI rules sources above | Survey statistics: cite the original survey and its sample |
| Risk & Compliance | Legislatures, regulators, gazettes, dockets for every place in the rules check | Bills vs enacted law; effective vs enforcement dates; court stays |
| People Analytics | BLS, OECD, Eurostat, original research reports | Methodology and sample size |
| L&D & Skills | Original reports from the publisher; vendor docs for product claims | Vendor-funded studies: name the funder |
| Career Pathways | BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, O*NET (onetonline.org) | Projection years and revisions |
| Mobility & Immigration | USCIS (uscis.gov), DHS, Federal Register; GOV.UK Home Office; IRCC (canada.ca) | High sensitivity: orientation only, absolute dates, no advice |
| HR Transformation | Company filings and announcements; original research | Analyst predictions are opinions: attribute them |
| Future of Work | Original research and official statistics | Forecasts vs measurements |
| Vendor Comparisons | Each vendor's documentation, trust and AI governance pages, audit reports | Every rating needs a dated source; re-verify quarterly |
