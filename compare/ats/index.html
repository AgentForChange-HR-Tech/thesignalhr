Best fit

Start from your situation, not the logo

You're a startup or scaleup moving fast

Ashby first; Gem if sourcing-led; Workable if budget rules.

You're mid-market and structure matters

Greenhouse first; Lever if pipeline/CRM is the center of your model.

You're enterprise with Workday as system of record

Workday Recruiting for one source of truth — accept the weight; iCIMS or SmartRecruiters if recruiting excellence outranks suite purity.

You hire high-volume hourly/frontline

Paradox — often alongside a core ATS rather than replacing it.

You're a staffing agency

Bullhorn. Corporate-focused platforms will fight your workflow.

You want talent intelligence beyond tracking

Eightfold — but treat it as a talent-intelligence layer decision, not an ATS swap.

The 10-question RFP bank

Questions that separate demo theater from deployed reality. Ask them in this order; the discomfort is diagnostic.

  1. Show me, live, how a recruiter moves a candidate from application to offer. Count the clicks.
  2. What does implementation actually require from my team, in hours per week, for how many weeks?
  3. Which of your AI features make or influence selection decisions — and where is your EU AI Act / NYC LL144 documentation for each?
  4. What's the all-in three-year cost for my volume — including add-ons, integrations, and the modules you assume I'll buy in year two?
  5. Which three integrations do your customers most regret assuming would 'just work'?
  6. What happens to my data — format, timeline, and cost — if I leave?
  7. Show me your analytics answering: where do my best hires come from, and where does my funnel leak?
  8. What did your last three price increases look like for existing customers at renewal?
  9. Who owns my account after go-live, and what's their book of business?
  10. What's on your roadmap that's dated and committed — versus directional?
