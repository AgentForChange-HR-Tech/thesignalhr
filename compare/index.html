How these comparisons are built

The method is the moat.

Independent — no vendor pays to appear, rank higher, or be described favorably; no affiliate links. Practitioner-led — drawn from hands-on enterprise systems experience, with every claim linked to its source. Dated — every comparison shows when it was last reviewed, because products and rules change.
