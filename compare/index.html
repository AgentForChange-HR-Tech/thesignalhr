How these comparisons are built

The method is the moat.

Independent — no vendor pays to appear, rank higher, or be described favorably; no affiliate links. Practitioner-led — drawn from hands-on enterprise systems experience, cross-checked against current analyst and review sources. Dated — every comparison shows when it was last reviewed, because stale advice is worse than none.
